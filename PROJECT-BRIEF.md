# Project Brief: Fable Growth

## Company & Product

Fable is a mental-wellness app for adults 25 to 45. It grew rapidly on a simple, calming experience and peaked at 4.2M active users — "the app that actually helped."

## The Situation

Growth has flatlined: the product works so well that users resolve their acute need within 90 to 120 days and quietly stop opening it.

Incumbents (Headspace, Calm) own brand awareness, and AI-native rivals are targeting Fable's core demographic.

## The Crisis

A retention crisis driven by its own success — the thing that made Fable work is the reason users leave.

## My Role & Goal

Product Leader at Fable, leading the **Fable Growth** initiative.

Goal: define what the product becomes after the acute phase, and build a durable engagement model that keeps high-value users beyond their first 90 days —

- without repositioning as a clinical platform
- without replacing the core content
